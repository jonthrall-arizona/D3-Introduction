D3.js · Data Visualization

D3 Introduction

Four charts built with D3.js, covering two real datasets: monthly UK driver fatalities from 1969–1984, and standardized test scores for a class of college seniors. This project marks an early step in learning D3 — later projects in this portfolio build on the techniques introduced here.

UK Driver Fatalities by Month, 1969–1984

Each cell is one month. Darker cells mark months with more driver fatalities, making the seasonal pattern — a rise every autumn and winter — easy to spot across the full 16-year span.

UK Driver Fatalities, Bubble View

The same monthly data, encoded a second way: bubble area scales with the fatality count for that month, from the lowest month (bottom of the legend) to the highest (top). Comparing this to the heatmap above shows two different ways of encoding the same variable.

UK Driver Fatalities Over Time

All 192 months plotted as a single time series. The seatbelt law introduced in early 1983 is visible as a sharp drop partway through the chart.

Calvin College Seniors: GPA vs. ACT Score

271 seniors, each plotted by GPA (x-axis) and ACT score (y-axis). Bubble size reflects SAT Verbal score; color reflects SAT Math score, from low (blue) to high (red).
